Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Reels Automation is a personal, self-hosted application used by its owner to prepare and publish videos through official platform APIs, including TikTok.

Information the application uses

The application may access account identifiers, authorization tokens, video files selected by its owner, captions, titles, hashtags, and publishing status returned by connected social platforms. This information is used only to perform the actions requested by the owner, such as uploading or checking the status of that owner’s content.

Storage and security

Tokens, publishing records, and video metadata are stored locally on the owner’s computer. Reels Automation does not operate a central service that collects or sells personal information. Access tokens are used only to call the official APIs that the owner has authorized.

Sharing

Information is sent only to the social platform selected by the owner when the owner requests a publishing or status operation. The application does not sell, rent, or share personal information with unrelated third parties.

Data deletion

The owner can revoke platform authorization through the relevant platform settings and can delete locally stored tokens, records, and media from the computer running the application.
